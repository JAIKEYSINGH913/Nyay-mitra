# 🚀 Revolutionizing Legal Accessibility with Nyay-Mitra: A Sovereign Judicial AI Engine

I am thrilled to announce the completion of my project, **Nyay-Mitra** — an industrial-grade, sovereign legal intelligence engine designed to bridge the gap between complex legal statutes and common citizens.

In a country where language barriers and legal complexity often deny justice, Nyay-Mitra provides a "glass-box" deterministic solution for the transition from legacy IPC to the new Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS 2023).

### 🔍 What is Nyay-Mitra?
Nyay-Mitra is a multilingual, multi-modal AI ecosystem that processes Indian legal data through four specialized neural layers:

1.  **🎙️ Nyay-Vani (Voice Intelligence)**: Allows users to query the law in 20+ Indian languages via voice. It transcribes, translates, and reasons through legal queries in real-time.
2.  **🌉 Nyay-Bridge (Statutory Mapping)**: A deterministic mapper that translates legacy IPC sections to new BNS provisions, highlighting deltas in punishments and legal scope.
3.  **🛡️ Nyay-Audit (Forensic Veracity)**: An anti-hallucination layer that audits legal documents, verifying citations against official gazettes to detect AI-generated errors.
4.  **🕸️ Nyay-Graph (Neural Discovery)**: A 3D Knowledge Graph powered by Neo4j that visualizes the interconnected web of statutes, precedents, and judicial reasoning.

### 🛠️ The Tech Stack
*   **Frontend**: Next.js 15, Framer Motion (Industrial Aesthetics), Three.js (3D Graph).
*   **Backend**: Unified FastAPI Gateway (Python), Java Spring Boot (Kernel).
*   **Intelligence**: Google Gemini 1.5 Pro, Sarvam AI (STT), Neo4j (Graph), Milvus (Vector Search).
*   **Infrastructure**: Fly.io (Backend), Vercel (Frontend), Appwrite (Auth).
